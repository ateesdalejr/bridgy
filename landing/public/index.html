Bridgy.chat

WhatsApp for people who would rather text.

Bridgy turns a basic SMS thread into a bridge for WhatsApp messages, so dumb phone users can stay reachable without living inside another app.

Built for the in-between

Keep the group chat without keeping the smartphone.

01

Text Bridgy

Send a normal SMS to your Bridgy number, including aliases like @maya.

02

Bridge to WhatsApp

Bridgy relays the message through the WhatsApp account you linked once.

03

Replies come back

WhatsApp replies land as SMS, ready for a basic phone or low-distraction setup.

The first beta

Small, text-only, and focused.

  • One linked WhatsApp account per SMS number.
  • Simple aliases for people you message often.
  • Designed for people testing dumb phones, Light Phones, or quieter routines.

Trust and control

Use the hosted bridge, or run your own.

Hosted Bridgy

No server, tunnel, webhook setup, or WhatsApp session storage to manage. Good for people who just want WhatsApp over SMS.

Self-hosted

The source is on GitHub under AGPL-3.0. Bring your own SMS provider, public HTTPS URL, and persistent disk.

Exit path

Text RESET to unlink WhatsApp locally. Text DELETE to delete Bridgy records for your SMS number.

Transparent by default

Messages are routed, not used for training. Diagnostic webhook previews are retained for seven days by default.
